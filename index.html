Our story

About Workbench

Workbench HR was built specifically for the businesses that keep Main Street running.

Main Street

The backbone of the American economy

Before there were global corporations, there were small businesses. Local shops, family-owned companies, skilled trades, and entrepreneurs built the communities we live in today. They created jobs, served their neighbors, and became the backbone of the American economy.

Today, more than 34 million small businesses continue that legacy, yet many are still expected to manage their people with spreadsheets, disconnected software, or enterprise HR systems designed for companies hundreds of times their size.

01

Our story

Why we built Workbench HR

After years of working alongside business owners through consulting engagements, one challenge appeared over and over again.

Owners were spending more time managing HR paperwork than growing their businesses.

Hiring a new employee meant searching for templates online. Performance conversations were documented in Word documents. Compliance deadlines lived on sticky notes, spreadsheets, or someone’s memory. Every new regulation created uncertainty, and many businesses simply could not justify the cost or complexity of enterprise HR platforms.

The problem wasn’t that these businesses didn’t care about their people.

The problem was that they didn’t have an HR department.

02

A new approach

Enterprise-quality HR without enterprise complexity

Workbench HR was created to bring enterprise-quality HR capabilities to small businesses without enterprise complexity.

Instead of disconnected tools, we built a single workbench where business owners can hire employees, generate documents, manage compliance, track performance, organize employee records, and receive intelligent guidance from an AI-powered HR Generalist.

Everything works together because that’s how HR actually works.

  • Hiring
  • Documents
  • Compliance
  • Performance
  • Employee records
  • AI HR Generalist
03

Built for owners

Built for the way small businesses actually operate

Most HR software was designed for large organizations with dedicated HR teams.

Workbench HR was designed for business owners.

Whether you’re running a cleaning company, HVAC business, construction firm, healthcare practice, law firm, real estate company, financial services firm, or growing technology startup, the goal is the same.

Spend less time managing paperwork.

Spend more time building your business.

04

The future of HR

People first. AI as support.

Artificial intelligence should not replace people.

It should make people better.

Our vision is to create the first AI-powered HR Generalist built specifically for Main Street businesses. One that helps owners navigate compliance, answer HR questions, generate documentation, recommend next steps, and reduce administrative work while keeping people at the center of every decision.

We believe every small business deserves access to the same quality HR support once reserved for large corporations.

That’s the future we’re building.
